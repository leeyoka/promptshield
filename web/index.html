System Features

The Three Pillars of PromptShield

A unified privacy and energy gateway engineered for individuals, freelancers, and small offices.

🔒

On-Device Privacy & Vault

Deterministic structural validators catch 16-digit Indonesian NIK (including province and +40 female birthdate offsets), Indonesian phone numbers, credit card Luhn checks, and Shannon entropy API keys. Raw data is encrypted with non-extractable AES-GCM-256 session keys.

  • Indonesian NIK region & date checks
  • In-browser Web Crypto AES-GCM encryption
  • Vault auto-cleared on session close
⚡

Seamless On-Screen Restoration

The AI chat provider only ever sees placeholder tokens such as [NIK_1] or [PHONE_1]. When the streamed response arrives, PromptShield's MutationObserver safely replaces placeholders in text nodes with your original values.

  • Handles chunked streaming boundaries
  • Text-node only (immune to href injection)
  • Reversible without degrading AI output quality
🌿

Energy Awareness & Right-Sizing

Every prompt consumes electrical power. Based on research from Google and Luccioni et al., a lightweight complexity heuristic classifies your prompt (Small/Medium/Large) and nudges you toward right-sized models, saving up to ~1 Wh per prompt.

  • Zero-latency mathematical heuristic
  • Visible energy savings per prompt
  • Cumulative carbon & energy footprint tracker
